Privacy & Cookies Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2025

We recognize that your privacy is very important and take it seriously. This Privacy & Cookies Policy describes TalentForge’s policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the services, websites, and applications offered by us (the “Services”). It also details your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

We only process your personal data and use cookies where we have a legal basis, including your consent where required under the GDPR and PIPEDA Directive. Your consent is requested via a cookie banner when you first visit our homepage. You may later withdraw or manage your consent via a Cookie Preferences link in the footer.

Capitalized terms not defined in this Privacy & Cookies Policy have the meanings given in our Terms of Service. This Policy is designed to meet transparency obligations under applicable law, including the GDPR and PIPEDA.

2. Your Rights Relating to Your Personal Data

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Delete or remove your data
  • Object to processing (where applicable)
  • Restrict certain types of processing
  • Portability of your data (GDPR/CCPA)
  • Withdraw consent previously given

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@talentforge.com. Requests are typically free unless clearly unfounded or repetitive. We may ask for identity verification. We aim to respond within one month; more complex requests may take longer. If you're in the EU and still unhappy, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., your country’s DPA).

3. Marketing Communications Preferences

You may opt-out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the “unsubscribe” link in emails
  • Adjusting your preferences in your account settings
  • Contacting privacy@talentforge.com

Opting out doesn’t affect communications essential for account, recruitment, or legal matters.

4. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our Services:

  • Identity & Contact Data: e.g., name, email, phone
  • Resume & Professional Data: e.g., work history, skills, preferences
  • Technical Data: e.g., IP address, browser, device info
  • Cookie & Usage Data: e.g., session info, site interaction

We do not collect sensitive personal data (e.g., government IDs, health).

Purpose Legal Basis

We retain your data as long as your account is active or required for legal reasons. When accounts are deleted, data is removed promptly and irrevocably from our databases. Render.com may retain server logs per their policies.

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. If a breach occurs, we’ll notify affected users and regulators as required.

Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn of such collection, we’ll delete it promptly.

We may update this policy at any time. We’ll update the “Last updated” date and post changes here. Significant changes will be communicated via email or site notice. It is important that the personal data we hold about you is accurate. Please keep us informed of any changes.
