Account Deletion Procedure

Effective Date: July 28, 2025
Owner: Privacy Officer
Version: 1.0
Applies To: All TalentForge user accounts (job seekers and recruiters)

1. Purpose

This document outlines the step-by-step procedure for processing user account deletion requests in accordance with our Privacy & Cookies Policy, and applicable data protection laws like GDPR, PIPEDA, and CCPA.

2. Scope

  • All registered users of TalentForge (Job Seekers and Recruiter Company Profiles)
  • User-initiated deletion via the TalentForge web application

3. Deletion Method

Users can delete their accounts at any time through their profile settings.

  1. Log in to your TalentForge account.
  2. Navigate to Profile.
  3. Click on “Delete My Account”.
  4. Confirm the deletion action when prompted.

4. Backend Process (Post-Deletion)

  1. The user’s account and associated data are immediately deleted from the database.
  2. This includes:
    • Personal information (name, email, profile details)
    • Uploaded resume or job listing details
    • Company/recruiter profile (if applicable)
  3. Session tokens and cookies are invalidated.
  4. A final confirmation email is sent to the user confirming account deletion.

5. Data Handling Notes

  • Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
  • No data is retained, unless required by law.
  • Render.com (our hosting provider) may retain server-level logs, which are not tied to user profiles and are managed per Render’s own retention policy.

6. Logging (Internal)

  • Account deletion is logged for audit purposes with:
    • Date and time of deletion
    • User ID or email
    • Method (UI-triggered)
  • No personally identifiable data is retained in the log beyond what’s required for compliance tracking.

7. Exceptions

There are no exceptions; all users can delete their own accounts. No admin approval is needed. Once deleted, accounts cannot be restored.

8. Review Cycle

This procedure will be reviewed annually or whenever a major change is made to the account deletion functionality.
